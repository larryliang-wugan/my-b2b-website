## Proposed payment and logistics — not yet approved factory policy

Recommend T/T 30% deposit and 70% after pre-shipment inspection, before dispatch, for initial standard orders; custom materials may require a different deposit confirmed in writing. Offer protected platform payment only when actually available. Do not promise open-account credit to new buyers.

Quote EXW or FCA for buyer-arranged collection, FOB named port for suitable ocean shipments, and DAP named destination for supported door delivery. Offer DDP only after confirming local importer, tax and customs feasibility. Use Incoterms 2020 and a named place/port. Show production lead time separately from transit time; no worldwide fixed freight or delivery guarantee.
