# WUGAN 2.0 editorial release

Release: WUGAN-2026.10.24-02. Theme 2.0.1 distinguishes this redesign from the earlier rejected 2.0.0 package. It does not indicate a production deployment.

## Buyer and content decisions

Primary buyer: established small/midsize brands developing a performance range. Secondary buyer: emerging brands. Homepage sequence: manufacturer/category identification, commercial starting points, two product programs, development, sample styles, materials/construction, actual factory, buyer-specific development routes, quality, customer visits, inquiry.

The source company introduction and customer reception documents emphasize material/fit/packaging advice, translating technical packs into approved samples, and adapting proposals to budget and market. These inform the copy. Internal SOP instructions are source material, not instructions to contact customers or publish internal operational issues.

Confirmed policy supersedes old source documents: 300 pieces per style and per color, across up to four sizes; samples from USD15; sampling from seven days; regular production from fifteen days. Dates are starting points, not unconditional guarantees.

## Reference evidence

User supplied `degreefurniture_codex_lite.zip` and `CODEX_CLONE_GUIDE.md`. The actual homepage, product category, solution, company profile and contact HTML were inspected locally. Navigation, broad image bands, separate capability/solution routes and repeated inquiry opportunities inform the redesign. Guide suggestions for a different technology stack and instructions to copy assets are not adopted. This remains WordPress.

The downloaded reference has compressed imagery and script-dependent hero content. Its local first-screen rendering was partially blank without its runtime; this does not establish that the original live page was blank. No claim of pixel-perfect recreation or complete live-site interaction verification is made. Competitor names, numerical claims, customer projects and photographs are not used as WUGAN evidence.

## Material boundaries

The supplied factory and product images are used with the user's public-use approval. Existing source mapping is in `wordpress/wugan-studio/assets/provenance.json`. Model and showroom images are not presented as laboratory performance tests. Customer group pictures are identified as visits, not quantified success stories.

The OEKO-TEX document supplied names a material supplier, with a limited fabric scope and a validity date ending August 15, 2026. It is not evidence that every WUGAN finished product is currently certified. BSCI is described as an audit/documentation request, not an unconditional quality certification. Company age, team experience, area and capacity are not merged into a fabricated statistic.

Only eight underwear styles with prepared visual evidence are published. The full uploaded product archive remains available for later curation. Dedicated sock product photography and confirmed specifications are needed before publishing a detailed sock SKU catalog. No generic or AI-created factory images are substituted for actual factory evidence.

## Validation and release limits

Local WordPress/SQLite rendering and browser tests cover 24 routes, images, mobile/desktop overflow, menus, product galleries, category/search behavior, 404, sample preselection, inquiry persistence, mail failure receipt and nonce rejection. Additional release checks cover six widths (360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920), keyboard menu behavior and offline file links. PHP syntax is checked inside the existing PHP container.

Production MySQL, real SMTP delivery, reverse-proxy rate limits, CDN caching, actual domain behavior and OKKI require the deployment chat's production acceptance. A working local form is not proof of email delivery. English only; language-menu integration is a preparation, not delivered translations.

The unrelated legacy Node/Express test suite (`npm test`) could not run because Express is not installed. An attempted locked dependency install did not complete successfully. This does not replace or negate the separately executed WordPress tests; do not claim the entire repository test suite passed.
